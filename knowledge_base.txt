International Travel Policy
Employees must receive approval from their department manager before booking international travel. All international flights must be booked through the approved company travel platform.


Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week. Remote work days require approval from the employee's direct manager.


Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive fifteen days of annual leave per year.


Expense Reimbursement Policy
Employees must submit valid receipts for reimbursable expenses. Expense claims should be submitted within thirty days.


Training Policy
Employees may request company-sponsored professional training with manager approval.


Information Security Policy
Employees must not share company passwords or confidential information with unauthorized parties.
